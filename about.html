Stranger Solemn

Solemn is a digital artist/producer and web3 experimentoor from Manchester, UK

creating works across multiple blockchains

Solemn's style leans heavily into dark glitch art, where the inherent chaos and disorder resonate deeply with his background in electronic music.

Artist's Statement

My work is an ongoing study of the digital artifact, born from a decades long evolution through sound, high-fidelity video, and eventually, the raw architecture of the blockchain. What began as a mastery of heavy mediums high-resolution video and complex GIFs has evolved through necessity into a custom form of technical minimalism.

The journey into art took me from the "dark glitch" Tezos scene of 2021 to a grueling year-long Everydays project in 2022, eventually leading to the brutal constraints of Bitcoin and the even stricter limits of Fully On-Chain (FOC) art on Ethereum. In my world, restrictions are ground for creativity.

My current style is a deliberate hybrid; a mixture of traditional 2D glitch art, GIFs, music, and live-rendered code. While it involves parts of hand-drawing, it is a much larger orchestration, a producer's landscape where every element is sequenced to work together. By utilizing the browser's own engine to render my pieces live, the art becomes dynamic; it does not "exist" as a pre-rendered image, but generates and performs only at the moment of viewing.

The result is a precision game where the code is the artwork from the inception.
