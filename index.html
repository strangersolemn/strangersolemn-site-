About

My creative journey is an ever-evolving exploration of computer-made art — visual, audio, animation, and code.

Before NFTs came along, my background was in the underground electronic music scene, wearing many creative hats: producer/DJ, graphic designer, videographer, and more. Music is my first love, and I try to include it in everything I do.

My NFT journey began in 2021, running from the dark-vibe Tezos scene through a year-long Everydays in 2022 — mostly high-fidelity GIFs and video at first. Arriving at Bitcoin at the advent of Ordinals changed that: the restraints of the network pushed me into creative coding and recursion.

My skills and tools keep evolving, and so does the work. My latest pieces on Ethereum and Bitcoin are built with code, music, and whatever the medium will allow — composed and arranged the way I'd produce a track, so it feels like I'm producing the art rather than just making it. I'm driven as much by innovation as by the craft itself, always reaching for something that hasn't been done before.

The journey continues…
