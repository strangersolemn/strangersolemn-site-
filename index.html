About

My creative journey is an ever-evolving exploration of computer-made art — visual, audio, animation, and code.

Before NFTs came along, my background was in the underground electronic music production scene, wearing many creative hats: producer/DJ, graphic designer, videographer, and more.

My NFT journey began in 2021, running from the dark-vibe Tezos scene through a year-long Everydays in 2022 — mostly high-fidelity GIFs and video at first. Arriving at Bitcoin at the advent of Ordinals changed that: the restraints of the network pushed me into creative coding and recursion.

Since then it's only kept evolving. My latest works on Ethereum and Bitcoin are composed with JavaScript libraries, music, and every tool I can get my hands on — every layer placed and sequenced until it plays as one… a bit like making a track.
