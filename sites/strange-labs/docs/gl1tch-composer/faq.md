# Questions

## Does it cost anything?

Using the tool is free. You only pay to inscribe the file you export, and the file is small.

## Can I sell what I make?

Yes. The gl1tches are CC0, so you can use them in anything, including work you sell. Your own art stays yours.

## Why does my piece have a white background?

It's part of that inscription's art, not something the composer added. Select the layer and set **Blend** to **Multiply**: the white drops out.

## Why is a layer blank or slow?

Every layer loads from Bitcoin by its ID. Big video or heavy HTML takes longer. Very new inscriptions can take a block or two to show up on ordinals.com.

## The link I saved, is it the newest version?

Yes. The tool lives on one sat, and every update is inscribed on that sat. The link always opens the newest one.

## Will my inscribed piece break if the CC0mposer changes?

No. Your piece points at the gl1tches and your art, not at the CC0mposer. Those inscriptions never change.

## Who made it?

Grimble built the first CC0mposer. Version 2 and the GL1TCH ENG1NE are by Solemn, part of [Strange Labs](https://www.strangersolemn.art/sites/strange-labs/).
