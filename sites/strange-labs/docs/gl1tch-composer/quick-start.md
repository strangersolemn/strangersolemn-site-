# Make your first piece

This takes about two minutes.

1. **Open the CC0mposer** from [Strange Labs](https://www.strangersolemn.art/sites/strange-labs/) or straight from [ordinals.com](https://ordinals.com/content/8e93bc148d9f8172ecb25e3831eaaf606ef932af7b146cf1c738084507302303i0). Check the switch at the top says **Inscribe**.
2. **Add a gl1tch.** Press **+ CC0 Gl1tch**. Tap a few in the picker to try them, then press **Use this one**.
3. **Shape it.** On the right, pick a colour, then move the sliders: speed, density, pixel size, RGB split.
4. **Add your art.** Paste the ID of any inscription into the box on the left and press Enter. It goes on top of the gl1tch.
5. **Blend it.** With your art selected, open **Blend** and try **Screen** (drops black backgrounds) or **Multiply** (drops white ones).
6. **Pick how it plays.** Under the picture: **All**, **Click**, **Block** or **Chance**. See [How it plays](playing.md).
7. **Export.** Press **Export**, then **Download .html**.
8. **Inscribe the file** with any inscribing tool. It runs straight away.

> **Tip:** to try one of Solemn's own pieces as your art, paste the skull from CORRUPTED:
> `8e37888454bb1a95a7e285200af99ac3a402b96fba0822cc608f49edb6c42163i0`
> It has a black background, so blend it with **Screen**.
