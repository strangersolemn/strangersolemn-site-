# Add your own art

Any inscription can be a layer: images, GIFs, SVG, video, HTML pieces and text.

## By inscription ID

1. Find the inscription's ID. It is 64 letters and numbers, then `i0` (or `i1`, `i2`…).
2. Paste it into the box on the left, under **CC0 Gl1tch** and **Inscription**. A full ordinals.com link works too.
3. Press Enter. It lands on the canvas, on top of the other layers.

Paste another to stack it on top. Use the **↑** and **↓** arrows in the layer list to change the order.

HTML pieces stay live: a piece that moves, reacts or reads the block height keeps doing it inside your composition.

## Inscribe mode and Demo mode

The switch at the top has two settings.

* **Inscribe** is the normal one. Layers come from inscriptions, by ID, so the file you export is ready to inscribe.
* **Demo** lets you drop files from your own computer onto the canvas, to try an idea before you inscribe anything. Don't inscribe a file exported in Demo mode: it points at files on your computer, which nobody else can see. Inscribe your art first, switch back to **Inscribe**, and add it by ID.

Switching between the two clears your own layers. The CC0 gl1tches stay.

## If it won't load

* "Doesn't look like an inscription ID": check it is the whole ID, ending in `i` and a number.
* "Couldn't find that inscription": it may be too new for ordinals.com to have it yet. Wait a block and try again.
* A white background you didn't expect is part of the art itself. Set the layer to **Multiply** to drop the white.
