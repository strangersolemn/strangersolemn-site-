Verify your Ordinals

Prove a Bitcoin wallet holds the work and get the Ordinals role in the Discord. You sign a message. Nothing is sent, nothing is spent, no transaction is ever asked for.

Step 1 · Discord

Type /verify in the Discord and open the link it gives you. The link is what connects this page to your Discord account.

Step 2 · Wallet

Connect the wallet that holds the work. Use the Ordinals address (the one starting bc1p).

Sign this exact message in your wallet (Sparrow, Xverse, Unisat all have "sign message"), then paste the address and the signature.
